Attrition was a promotion problem

A field sales force loses people everywhere. It loses the most under team leaders who were promoted for their own sales numbers. This page shows the joined records, the checks, and what doing nothing costs in sales.

The quick read points at regions

Ranked by region, turnover looks like a location problem. But location and leadership overlap: the branches that lose the most people also have more teams under top-seller leaders. The next section separates the two.

Exits by how the team leader was promoted

No record links a seller to a leader, and none records promotions. The leader comes from the center code both share. The route comes from the leader's own job-title history: a Sales Officer in the top fifth of their branch before becoming Team Leader counts as a top seller.

The exit form says growth, not leadership

Few people tick "Leadership" on an exit form, and fewer than half fill one in. Read alone, the form clears the leaders. The joined view does not.
